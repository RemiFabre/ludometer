The games, and what was changed

azul  perfect information · no luck after the draft · unsolved

Azul (Kiesling 2017): the calibrated deep reference.

uno  hidden hands · high luck · unsolved

Uno, official 2-player rules (draw only when stuck, no stacking). First to 500 points wins.

unoplus  hidden hands · high luck · unsolved

Uno+ = Uno plus four house rules. First to 500 points wins.
  • 9-card deal (instead of 7).
  • Drawing a card is always a legal move (up to a 15-card hand cap).
  • +2 and +4 stack, like on like: instead of drawing, you may answer a +2 with your own +2 (or a +4 with a +4), passing the grown penalty back - whoever runs out of answers draws the whole stack.
  • Playing a 7 swaps hands (both players then know the opposing hand exactly).
Note: with the default Uno rules ~64% of turns are forced; with the Uno+ rules that drops to ~19%.

tictactoe  perfect information · no luck · solved (draw)

Tic-tac-toe: a solved game used as calibration. The agent reaches perfect play very rapidly, as expected.

connect4  perfect information · no luck · solved (first player wins)

Connect Four: tic-tac-toe's character, but a much deeper game.

lostcities  hidden hand · high luck · unsolved · Knizia 1999

Lost Cities: Uno's dials on a great game's design - the control case. The rules in four lines: on your turn play one card onto one of your five colour expeditions (ascending only) or discard it, then draw from the deck or a discard pile. Starting an expedition costs 20 points; its cards earn them back. Handshake cards played first multiply the colour's result. The game ends when the deck runs out; higher total wins.
